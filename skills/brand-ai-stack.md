# Formation Brand AI Stack — Skill File
*Load this when creating ANY content for Formation: reading plans, hub pages, emails, app copy, notifications, social posts.*

---

## CORE RULE: AI Expands. Tony Curates. Never Publish Raw AI.

Formation's competitive moat is Tony's voice and scholarship. Every piece of AI-generated content must be filtered through Tony's perspective before publishing. The Brand AI Stack defines WHEN and HOW AI is used at each stage.

---

## THE 5-STAGE BRAND AI STACK

### Stage 1 — Seed (Tony Only)
Tony provides the creative seed: a scripture passage, a theological insight, a series theme, a reader pain point.
- AI does NOT create the seed.
- The seed is the thing that makes Formation different from every other app.

**Example seeds:**
- "The Ethiopian Bible includes the Book of Enoch — most Christians have never heard of it."
- "The word 'ekklesia' meant something specific in Greek that got lost in translation."
- "Why did the Council of Laodicea remove certain books?"

### Stage 2 — Expansion (AI)
AI takes the seed and expands it: suggests related scriptures, outlines a 7-day reading plan structure, generates 3 possible headline angles, drafts a first email in the welcome sequence.

**Prompt structure for expansion:**
```
You are building content for Formation Bible Study, a Bible study app for serious 
students who want depth, not devotionals. The brand voice is: scholarly but accessible, 
direct, truth-seeking, never preachy. The reader identity is: "I want the whole truth, 
not the edited version."

Seed: [TONY'S SEED]
Task: [expand into / draft / outline / suggest]
Format: [reading plan / hub page / email / push notification]
```

### Stage 3 — Curation (Tony)
Tony reviews the AI expansion and selects what's accurate, what's on-brand, what's cut.
- Delete anything that sounds generic, corporate, or like a church website
- Keep anything that reinforces the truth-seeker identity
- Mark passages or ideas that need deeper theological verification

### Stage 4 — Voice Filter (AI with Voice Rules)
AI rewrites the selected content through the Formation voice filter (see Voice Rules below).

### Stage 5 — Final Approval (Tony)
Tony does final read. Any sentence that doesn't feel like Tony wrote it gets rewritten.

---

## FORMATION VOICE RULES

**The reader is:** A serious, curious adult who suspects they were given an incomplete picture of scripture. They are not hostile to faith — they want more of it, not less. They are allergic to hype, preachy language, and shallow devotionals.

**Tone:** Direct. Scholarly but not academic. Warm but not soft. Confident, not arrogant.

**Do say:**
- "Here's what most Bible apps won't show you."
- "The Greek word here is [word]. And it changes everything."
- "This passage has been mistranslated in most English Bibles. Here's why it matters."
- "You deserve the whole picture."

**Never say:**
- "Begin your faith journey today!"
- "Let God transform your heart."
- "Community of believers coming together."
- "Grow in your walk with the Lord."
- "Devotional" (use "study" or "passage" instead)
- "Blessed" (except in direct scripture quotes)
- Anything a church bulletin would say

## CONTENT TEMPLATES

### Reading Plan Template
```
TITLE: [Bold claim about what the reader will discover]
Subtitle: [7-Day / 14-Day / 30-Day] Study in [Book/Topic]

INTRO (2–3 sentences):
[What the reader will learn that they probably didn't know. Lead with the surprising insight, not the plan structure.]

DAY [N] — [Day Title: one specific thing discovered today]
PASSAGE: [Book Chapter:Verse-Verse] (BSB)
[The passage text]
CONTEXT: [2–3 sentences of historical/cultural context Tony would know]
OBSERVATION: [What do you notice in the text?]
INTERPRETATION: [What does it mean — specifically, not generically]
APPLICATION: [One concrete action, decision, or mindset shift]
REFLECTION QUESTION: [One specific, thought-provoking question]
```

### Series Hub Page Template
```
HEADLINE: [The surprising truth this series reveals]
SUBHEAD: [Who this series is for + what they'll walk away knowing]

[2-sentence hook: the conventional understanding vs. what this series reveals]

EPISODE LIST:
Ep. 1 — [Title]
Ep. 2 — [Title]
[etc.]

WHAT YOU'LL DISCOVER: [3-bullet list — each bullet is a specific surprising insight]

RELATED READING PLAN: [Link to companion reading plan]

COMMON QUESTIONS: [3-5 Q&As — this is the schema-structured FAQ section]

BOOK PROMO BLOCK: [Standard promo block from MASTER_SKILL.md]
```

### 3-Part Email Welcome Sequence
```
EMAIL 1 (Immediate post-signup):
Subject: You're in. Here's what Formation actually is.
[What makes Formation different from every other Bible app — one specific example]
[The truth-seeker identity: "You're not here for a daily verse. You're here for the whole picture."]
CTA: Start your first free reading plan → [link]

EMAIL 2 (Day 3):
Subject: Most people never knew this was in the Bible.
[One surprising fact from Tony's scholarship — Ethiopian Bible, Dead Sea Scrolls, etc.]
[Bridge to: "This is the kind of thing Formation is built to help you discover."]
CTA: Explore the [Series Name] series → [link]

EMAIL 3 (Day 7):
Subject: The reading plan that changes how you read everything.
[The outcome: what a Formation user is able to do after completing one plan]
[The book promo: "Ready to go even deeper?"]
CTA: [Waitlist CTA or App Download CTA]
```

### Push Notification Copy Framework
```
Rule 1: Never sound like a reminder. Sound like a discovery.
❌ "Don't forget your daily reading!"
✅ "Day 3 of your Dead Sea Scrolls plan — today's passage is one most people never see."

Rule 2: Use the specific, not the generic.
❌ "Continue your Bible study."
✅ "The word 'ekklesia' in today's passage — here's what it really meant."

Rule 3: Trigger curiosity, not guilt.
❌ "You're 3 days behind on your plan."
✅ "You left off at something interesting. Ready to see where it goes?"
```

## AI TOOL ASSIGNMENTS

| Task | Recommended Approach |
|------|---------------------|
| First-draft reading plan entries | Claude Sonnet (depth, nuance) |
| Headline options (5–10 variations) | Claude Haiku (fast, high volume) |
| Hub page SEO FAQ answers | Claude Sonnet + schema formatting |
| Email subject line variations | Claude Haiku (test 5–10 at once) |
| App microcopy (button labels, empty states) | Claude Haiku |
| Theological fact-checking | Always Tony — AI hallucinates scripture |
| Voice/tone review | Run final draft through MASTER_SKILL.md filter |
