The Problem

The Solution

How It Works

Study the Bible the Way It Was Meant to Be Studied

Formation teaches you HOW to study, not just where to click. You'll follow the same method serious theologians use — made simple.

1

Start Where You Are

A short onboarding assessment places you at the right level — New Believer, Growing Christian, or Mature Student. No judgment. Just the right tools.

2

Follow the Framework

Every passage guides you through Observation (what does it say?), Interpretation (what does it mean?), and Application (how does it change me?).

3

Go Deeper as You Grow

Unlock cross-references, Strong's concordance, and full commentaries as your maturity grows. The tools appear when you're ready for them.

4

Study With Others

Join a study group, find an accountability partner, or discuss passages with your small group — real community, not just public sharing.
