Why "reviewed," not generated on the fly

Every guide in this library was written and checked before it was published — not generated fresh each time someone loads the page. That distinction matters for a study resource: a static, reviewed guide says the same true thing to every reader, every time, and can be corrected once if something needs fixing rather than drifting from one answer to the next. Each guide is checked against the underlying Berean Standard Bible text and, where a claim about history, language, or a named source is made, against a real published reference — not generated fresh from a prompt with no way to verify it.

That's different from Ask the Word, the in-app feature built for your own specific question in the moment — grounded in the same sourced evidence, but generated live around whatever you actually ask. Guides for the common questions; Ask the Word for the question only you have.
