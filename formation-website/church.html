What it is

A companion for reading, reflection, and conversation — not a curriculum or a replacement for pastors. The English app uses the Berean Standard Bible. Ask the Word answers from Scripture only; check its responses against the biblical text.

A sample study: Psalm 23

This is the kind of passage-and-questions study a group member might read between meetings. The questions below were written by our team as a sample. They are not output from Ask the Word.

Passage · Berean Standard Bible

1 The LORD is my shepherd;
I shall not want.

2 He makes me lie down in green pastures;
He leads me beside quiet waters.

3 He restores my soul;
He guides me in the paths of righteousness
for the sake of His name.

4 Even though I walk through the valley of the shadow of death,
I will fear no evil,
for You are with me;
Your rod and Your staff, they comfort me.

5 You prepare a table before me
in the presence of my enemies.
You anoint my head with oil;
my cup overflows.

6 Surely goodness and mercy will follow me
all the days of my life,
and I will dwell in the house of the LORD
forever.

Berean Standard Bible, public domain.

Sample questions for reflection

  1. Observe. In verses 1–3, what does the shepherd do for the one who follows him? What changes in verse 4, when David says “You” instead of “He”?
  2. Reflect. Where do you see rest, guidance, protection, and provision in this psalm?
  3. Discuss. Which line is hardest to trust in a season you, or someone you know, is facing? What would it look like to bring that line to the group?

How a leader might use it with a group

Keep the passage and meeting format you already use. Interested adults can read the passage between meetings, note their questions, and bring them back to the group. You decide whether this companion is useful; participation remains optional.
