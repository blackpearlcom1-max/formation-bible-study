Ask the Word
shows its work.

Ask the Word is an AI assistant that starts from the Bible text. Ask it any question and it answers from Scripture's perspective, and tells you plainly when the Bible does not speak directly to it. When your question names a passage, the answer is built from that passage in the Berean Standard Bible. Anything it says from outside the text, like history, tradition, or Greek and Hebrew, is labeled so you know it has not been checked.

Under the Hood

How it works, step by step

No mystery. This is exactly what happens when you ask a question.

1

You ask a question in plain language

Type it the way you'd ask a trusted pastor — "What does John 1:1 mean when it says 'the Word was God'?" "What is the connection between Passover and the Last Supper?" or even an everyday question like "How should I think about work stress?" Ask the Word answers it through Scripture, and says so when the Bible does not speak directly to it.

2

The passage text is fetched when you name a reference

If your question names a Bible reference, such as John 1:1, the app fetches that passage from the Berean Standard Bible. The text comes first, and the answer is built from it. If no reference is named, the AI answers from its general training under the same rules.

3

The AI writes the answer, and a review runs

Claude drafts an answer from that text. A safety check then reviews the draft. The verse references an answer cites are checked against the Bible text; quoted wording may be paraphrased. If the app cannot give a reliable answer from the Bible text, it says so instead of guessing.

4

You get an answer with its scope stated

The answer says what it is based on. Any claims about history, tradition, original languages, or scholars' views appear in a separate block labeled "Not checked here", outside the answer. Your study stays yours.

Trust & Safety

What it will
never do

  • block

    Pass off outside claims as checked Scripture

    Only the verse references an answer cites are checked against the Bible text. History, tradition, original-language claims and scholars' views are not, and they are always labeled "Not checked here".

  • block

    Give medical, legal or financial instructions

    You can ask about any topic. For medical, legal or financial questions, Ask the Word recommends a qualified professional and then shows what Scripture says. It does not tell you what to decide.

  • block

    Claim a seal of approval

    Answers carry no "approved" or "certified" badge. Interpretation is not guaranteed to be right, and the Bible text is the authority.

  • block

    Call any group of people cursed or inferior

    It is built to decline claims that a group is cursed or inferior, or that Scripture is a racial map. Some sensitive topics use fixed, careful wording, and certain claims are blocked outright.

  • block

    Replace your pastoral community

    Ask the Word is a study tool, not your elder board. For life decisions, theological disputes, and pastoral counsel, go to your church. If a topic like self-harm or abuse comes up, it shows support resources, which are not part of the Bible check.

Common Questions

Still have questions?

Does Ask the Word use ChatGPT or a general AI? expand_more
It does not use ChatGPT. Ask the Word uses Anthropic's Claude model with Formation's rules for how to answer. When your question names a Bible reference, the answer is built from that passage in the BSB. It can still misread a question or the text, so check the passage yourself.
What if Ask the Word gets something wrong? expand_more
Every response should be measured against the text of Scripture itself, and that is how Formation is meant to be used. AI can be wrong, and the app does not check history, tradition, original-language claims, or scholars' views. If you find a response that seems inaccurate, flag it in the app so we can review it. If the app cannot give a reliable answer from the Bible text, it says so instead of guessing.
Is this safe for new or young believers? expand_more
It can be, with caveats. Ask the Word is an AI and can be wrong, and it may give nuanced answers on difficult passages. Some sensitive topics use fixed, careful framing. We encourage all users, especially newer believers, to bring what they find to their pastor or small group. Formation is designed to deepen your engagement with Scripture and your community, not replace either one.
