## 🧠 Core Marketing Intelligence (from Reports)

### The Big Shift: Human Search → AI Agents
By 2026, AI agents are the new "customer." They research, recommend, and purchase on behalf of users. Formation must be the source these agents cite when someone asks:
- *"What's the best Bible study app?"*
- *"How do I do inductive Bible study?"*
- *"What Bible app should I use for deeper study?"*

### The 6 Actionable Priorities for Formation

| # | Strategy | Formation Application |
|---|----------|----------------------|
| 1 | **GEO & AEO** — Optimize for AI citation, not just ranking | Structure all website content with direct Q&A answers AI can cite |
| 2 | **Build for Citation & Trust** | Publish authoritative content on inductive Bible study, Berean Standard Bible, theology |
| 3 | **Omnichannel Presence** | Be on YouTube, TikTok, ChatGPT knowledge, Perplexity, and Claude |
| 4 | **Cost Per Closed Deal (not per lead)** | Track which channels produce paying subscribers, not just signups |
| 5 | **LinkedIn for B2B** | Target pastors, churches, and ministry orgs (bulk licenses) |
| 6 | **New Metrics** | Track AI citation frequency + agent retrieval, not just clicks/rankings |

### Key Insight for Bible App Niche
Users searching for spiritual tools have **high purchase intent** — this is exactly the audience where AI-referred traffic converts at 10-11% (B2C revenue). Formation is well-positioned to dominate AI citations in the Christian/Bible study vertical because it is a **niche with low AI-citation competition**.
